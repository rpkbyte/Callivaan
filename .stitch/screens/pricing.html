Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Callivaan billing, numbers, and minutes.

What happens when I exhaust my included minutes? expand_more
Your calls will never drop or get blocked abruptly mid-conversation. You can either enable automatic overage billing at the standard transparent per-minute rate for your tier, or configure a fixed top-up safety limit in your Android control hub.
Can I pay using UPI AutoPay or Indian corporate cards? expand_more
Yes. We integrate Razorpay Subscriptions supporting UPI recurring mandates (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), all major Indian debit/credit cards (RuPay, Visa, Mastercard), and Netbanking for verified business accounts.
Can I cancel or switch plans anytime? expand_more
Yes. You can cancel your subscription with a single tap in the app without penalty or retention calls. Upgrades or downgrades between tiers apply immediately, with billing adjusted on a pro-rata basis.
Do you provide a GST invoice for input tax credit? expand_more
Absolutely. Enter your 15-digit GSTIN during checkout or inside your billing settings, and automated tax invoices with B2B input credit will be delivered directly to your registered email and WhatsApp every cycle.
Do I need any specialized hardware or PBX equipment? expand_more
Zero hardware required. Callivaan connects through simple call forwarding (conditional when busy/unanswered or unconditional 24/7) from your existing mobile SIM or landline number. Setup takes less than 2 minutes.

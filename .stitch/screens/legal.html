SECTION 01 Last updated: Oct 2026

Core Terms & Platform Service Boundaries

These Terms of Service constitute a legally binding contractual framework between Callivaan AI Telephony Technologies Private Limited ("Callivaan", "we", "our") and the enterprise or proprietor utilizing our automated voice telephony infrastructure ("Customer", "Subscriber").

1.1 Scope of Telephony Receptionist

Callivaan provides an automated AI-assisted voice receptionist that interfaces with regular carrier inbound voice streams to answer queries, record appointment intent, relay store schedules, and summarize prospective client data into connected dashboard ledgers and WhatsApp dispatch queues.

emergency_home

Explicit Non-Emergency Telecommunications Disclaimer

Callivaan Voice AI is engineered solely for commercial appointments, operational inquiry triage, and business communications. Callivaan is NOT an emergency dispatch network, PSAP terminal, or public safety provider. Subscribers must never market or direct callers to Callivaan numbers for life-critical, police, fire, ambulance, or hazardous emergencies. Caller lines requiring immediate rescue should dial National Emergency Number 112, Police 100, Fire 101, or Health Emergencies 108 directly.

1.2 Business Knowledge Integrity & Agent Autonomy

The voice agent speaks strictly within the operational boundaries, product lists, pricing catalogs, and operating hours provided by the business account administrator. Callivaan disclaims liability for any unverified promises made outside explicit knowledge-base constraints if the subscriber intentionally inputs erroneous data into the prompt engine.

SECTION 02 TRAI Consent Protocol

Privacy Policy, Audio Consent & Call Summaries

We treat caller audio with stringent cryptographic discipline. Under Indian digital data frameworks, audio waveforms constitute sensitive data and are governed by strict recording and summarization standards.

mic_off

Default Audio State: Disabled

Full dual-channel call recording is disabled by default across all newly provisioned accounts. Only speech-to-text semantic tokens are processed in volatile memory.

record_voice_over

Mandatory Audio Disclaimer

When recording is activated by the subscriber, an unskippable prompt ("This call is answered by an automated voice assistant and recorded for service quality") executes before caller speech processing.

2.1 Structured Lead Extraction & Minimization

Callivaan algorithms isolate only relevant operational attributes from caller dialogue to deliver structured WhatsApp notifications:

  • Caller Identifier & Name (as verbally stated or resolved via carrier header)
  • Requested Service, SKU, or Appointment Date and Time Window
  • Call Urgency Level and Caller Summary Note
verified Zero Third-Party PII Monetization Guarantee

3.1 Hardware Interconnect Verification

Callivaan utilizes official enterprise SIP trunk gateways supplied by authorized Indian telecom licensees. We do not use, sell, or deploy unauthorized GSM fixed-cellular terminals ("SIM boxes") or bypass lawful interception interfaces defined by the Ministry of Communications.

3.2 Sovereign Cloud Residency (DPDP Act Compliance)

In adherence to the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023), 100% of our voice ingestion pipelines, automatic speech recognition inference clusters, and client database storage instances are located strictly in AWS Mumbai Region (ap-south-1). No telephone conversation or raw transcription leaves the sovereign borders of India.

SECTION 04 Retention Standard

Data Retention & Right-to-be-Forgotten

We believe in bounded retention schedules. Storing inactive customer communications creates unnecessary organizational risk. Our retention architecture operates on three core commitments:

90-Day Purge Window

Detailed call logs, caller transcripts, and audio recordings are systematically wiped after 90 days.

One-Click Cryptographic Wipe

Subscribers can purge any caller record or complete history from their portal instantly with zero residue.

Free Data Portability

Export complete structured lead ledgers at any time in industry-standard CSV and JSON files at no charge.

Any caller requesting total erasure of their telephone interaction under Indian DPDP rights can request an expedited purge through their business counterparty or via our statutory DPO desk at legal@callivaan.com.

SECTION 05 Billing & Subscriptions

Subscription, Cancellation & Refund Policy

Callivaan provides pre-billed monthly and annual SaaS licensing tiers. All subscriptions operate with clear usage boundaries and zero surprise drops.

5.1 Included Minutes & Grace Overages

Every plan (Starter, Business, Multi-Location) includes a pre-allocated bundle of inbound answered telephony minutes per month. If a high-volume call surge exceeds your bundle, Callivaan does NOT sever in-progress customer phone calls. Overage minutes are accrued transparently at published standard tier rates and invoiced on your subsequent cycle.

5.2 Cancellation Guarantee

Subscribers may cancel recurring billing at any point directly within the Control Hub settings dashboard with a single click. Cancellation becomes effective immediately at the close of the current paid billing period; no subsequent renewal charges will execute.

5.3 GST Tax Compliance & Invoicing

Callivaan generates GST-compliant e-invoices with designated HSN/SAC codes (SAC Code: 998431 - Cloud Telephony & Information Technology Services). Indian registered businesses may input their GSTIN identifier to claim legitimate Input Tax Credit (ITC) under GST laws.

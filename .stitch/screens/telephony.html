Carrier Troubleshooting

Ensure your SIM card has an active unlimited calling pack or sufficient main balance. Carrier call forwarding relies on standard voice outgoing routing rules.

Forwarded minutes utilize the standard unlimited voice calling quota bundled with your normal Jio, Airtel, or Vi prepaid/postpaid plan. No special VAS fees apply.

Ensure you dial the shortcode explicitly using the physical SIM slot assigned to your business number (+91 98765 43210), not your secondary personal SIM.

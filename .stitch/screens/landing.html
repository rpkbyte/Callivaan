Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about setting up Callivaan for your Indian business.

Do I need to change my current business phone number? expand_more
No. You keep your existing mobile number or landline. Callivaan connects using standard conditional call forwarding supported natively by Reliance Jio, Airtel, Vodafone Idea (Vi), and BSNL. Whenever your line is busy, unanswered after 3 rings, or out of network, calls automatically forward to your Callivaan AI agent.
How does Callivaan handle Hindi and English mixed sentences (Hinglish)? expand_more
Callivaan's acoustic and natural language pipeline is specifically fine-tuned on real Indian speech patterns. When a customer speaks conversational Hinglish (e.g., "Bhaiya aaj shaam ko 6 baje haircut ka appointment mil sakta hai kya?"), the model understands the intent seamlessly and responds in natural, courteous tone without awkward translation pauses.
Can I listen to call recordings and review audio transcripts? expand_more
Yes. Every call produces a full bilingual text transcript and an optional 128kbps audio recording. You can listen directly within the Android APK or click the recording link delivered with your WhatsApp notification message.
How fast does the lead notification arrive on my WhatsApp? expand_more
Our cloud pipeline processes and summarizes the conversation within 800 milliseconds of the caller hanging up. The structured WhatsApp message typically arrives on your phone before the customer even locks their screen.
Can I test the agent with my own phone before going live? expand_more
Absolutely. During the 14-day free trial, you receive a sandbox testing number. You can dial in, simulate customer inquiries, change instructions via WhatsApp, and ensure you are 100% satisfied before enabling call forwarding on your commercial lines.
